# Visitor / Prospect Persona

**Persona**: Visitor, Prospect  
**Aliases**: Unauthenticated user, prospect, anonymous visitor  
**Source**: [docs/USER_PERSONAS.md](../../docs/USER_PERSONAS.md#persona-0-visitor--prospect)

---

## Overview

A **Visitor** is someone who has not yet authenticated or registered. They may land on a public landing page, documentation, or health endpoint, or be redirected to the login page. Once they register or log in, they assume a role-based persona.
