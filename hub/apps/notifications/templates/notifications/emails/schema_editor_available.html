What's changed for your tenant:

  • Contracts that have no resolvable models or schema fields can now be fixed in-product without a YAML/JSON round trip.

What we recommend

  1. Open Contract Health.
  2. For each row, click Open Schema editor and add at least one model with one field.
  3. Save. The editor uses optimistic concurrency (ETag / If-Match) so a teammate's concurrent edit will surface a conflict dialog, not silently overwrite your changes.
